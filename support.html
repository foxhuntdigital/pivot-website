Common questions

The things people ask most.

01

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple. Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, select PIVOT and cancel there. Cancelling before a free trial ends means you are not charged. We cannot cancel a subscription on your behalf.

02

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled entirely by Apple under Apple's terms, because Apple takes the payment. Request one through Apple's "Report a Problem" page or your purchase history in Settings. Payment never passes through us, so we never see your card details and cannot issue a refund directly.

03

How do I delete my account and data?

Email support@YOUR-DOMAIN.com from the address on your account and ask for deletion. We remove your account and the training, wellbeing and coach conversation data associated with it. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — cancel that in your Apple ID settings as well.

04

Why does heart rate show as "not connected"?

PIVOT does not read from HealthKit, wearables or heart-rate monitors. There is no integration to connect, so that field stays empty by design. Readiness is built from the sessions you log and the inputs you enter.

05

My plan changed after I missed a session.

That is the engine working. Missing a session does not put you behind — the remaining work is redistributed around your race date. If a change does not make sense to you, ask Coach why, or write to us with the date and we will look at it.

06

I only have twenty minutes today.

Use the Micro version of the session. Every session exists in Full, Express and Micro, so a short day keeps the training stimulus rather than being skipped.
