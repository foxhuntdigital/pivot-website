Adaptive training for hybrid events

The plan rebuilds itself around your actual week.

PIVOT is a training app for hybrid event athletes. Instead of handing you a fixed calendar and letting it fall apart by Wednesday, it recalculates around your race date, the time you really have, and the work you actually completed.

App Store badge How it works

What you get

Four things, done properly.

01

An adapting plan

Your plan is rebuilt around your race date, your week, and what you actually completed. Miss a session and the engine redistributes the work rather than leaving a hole in the calendar. Add a constraint and the following weeks change to match.

02

Full, Express and Micro

Every session exists in three lengths. When a day collapses to twenty minutes, you run the Micro version and keep the training stimulus, instead of skipping the day and losing it entirely.

03

Coach

Ask why a session is what it is, and get the reasoning behind it. Change it in a sentence — shorter, no running, heavier — and the plan updates around the change.

04

Readiness

Six measures drawn from your own training, each shown with the confidence behind it. You can see which numbers the engine is sure about and which ones it is still learning, so you know how much weight to give them.

Readiness

Measured from your own work.

Readiness is built from the sessions you logged and the inputs you entered. Nothing is read from a wearable, and nothing is inferred from anyone else's training.

Training load

What you have actually done across recent weeks.

Recovery inputs

Sleep and energy, as you reported them.

Session effort

Your own RPE after each session.

Consistency

How completed work compares to what was planned.

Race proximity

Where you sit relative to your race date.

Confidence

How much data sits behind each measure.
